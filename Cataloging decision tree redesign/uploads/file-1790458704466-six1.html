    Glossary

    Bibliographic record
    A record that describes a title (book, journal, video, etc.) with information like author, title, publisher, and subject
    Community Zone (CZ)
    Ex Libris's global shared catalog containing bibliographic records and a Central Knowledge Base (CKB) of electronic portfolios and collections
    Electronic collection
    A group of electronic titles from a vendor, such as a database or package
    Electronic portfolio
    The electronic equivalent of holdings; links your library to an individual e-resource title
    Holdings
    A record attached to a bibliographic record that says "this library owns this item," including location and call number
    Institution Zone (IZ)
    Your institution's local catalog and holdings; only your library sees these records
    Item record
    A record attached to a holdings record for a specific physical copy, including barcode and material type
    Linked copy
    When you add holdings to an NZ or CZ record, Alma creates a copy in your IZ that stays connected to the shared record
    Metadata Editor
    Alma's built-in tool for viewing and editing catalog records
    Network Zone (NZ)
    CUNY's shared catalog; records here are visible to all CUNY libraries
    OCLC number
    A unique record ID from WorldCat
